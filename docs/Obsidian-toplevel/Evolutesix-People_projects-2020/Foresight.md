**Application key elements**
We are facing a polycrisis threatening the future of humanity; perhaps the next Toba event 70k years ago, where we again have only a few thousand breeding pairs of humans alive. My focus is on the fundamental reasons why our economy is net negative rather than net positive.

The deepest root is the 350 year old flaw in economics: assuming that capital processes in business are ergodic; however, they are non-ergodic. Mathematically non-ergodic capital dynamics strictly consumes more resources for a given growth rate than ergodic. This flawed assumption must be corrected for any net positive, regenerative, circular, ESG, etc. endeavour to have any hope of success. Else it will still be like carrying water in a colander.

  

Couple the flawed assumption of ergodicity with human nature has led to our current business paradigm prizing pure competition, the exclusion of non-financial capitals from business structures and governance, attributing merit as the cause of outcomes actually due to unpredictable randomness in non-ergodic processes; and rejecting the power of blended collaboration and competition that enables nature to thrive even in times of rapid change.

  

The non-ergodic nature of capital growth makes clear that the best individual strategy for success is through collaboration, specifically a level of capital pooling. Nature does this for biomass growth, traditional cultures and religions do this (e.g. the tithe) for all capitals relevant to human success. The flawed thinking in economics has led us to mistakenly believe collaboration is based only on values or ethics. Actually it has an anchor in the pure numbers.

  

My key project is fixing, and providing the open-source tools for everyone to rebuild business accordingly. My approach fixes the ergodicity flaw across five ecosystems: how businesses form local economies; how stakeholders and capitals (all six) form individual businesses; how businesses operate; how teams interact; and each of us as an individual ecosystem.
