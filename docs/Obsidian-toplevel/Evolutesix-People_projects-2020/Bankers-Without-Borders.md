**Application**
- Describe your company, including the number of people in your team and the problem you solve.
  Evolutesix is a holding company and venture builder dedicated to building and investing in complete ecosystems of regenerative, circular, and net positive companies. Problem: One of the key barriers to circularity, regeneration, etc. lies in how we build and run companies, as well as how we invest in companies. Seen in the first-mover disadvantage in circular startups, in 50% of VC funds delivering <1x returns to their LPs, in Paul Polman's challenges getting Unilever antifragile net positive; in short, why haven't the best in the world, with half a century of warning, been unable to build a regenerative economy? We currently have 3 full time staff, 7 volunteers, 8 startups in our ecosystem already. 
- How is your solution uniquely adressing the problem? 
  Evolutesix uniquely solves three classes of problems. 1) the flawed implicit assumption of ergodicity pervasive in economics and investing, which systemically increases the probabilities of startups succeeding across all their metrics, including investor ROI and capital preservation for investors. We do this by investing via an ecosystem / whole circle level holding company deploying a fractional profit pool to take us closer to the ergodic maximum. See my book "The Ergodic Investor and Entrepreneur". 2) Incorporating excluding key capitals and stakeholders is a key barrier. We incorporate companies as a FairShares Commons, including key capitals and stakeholders in governance and in a share of the wealth generated. 3) We use sociocracy and our deliberately developmental approach to turn the tensions at work into the organisational and human developmental outcomes needed to deliver the business outcomes with less effort.
- How does your solution benefit the environment and promote social good?
  We deliver environmental and social good because we remove the system barriers to doing so in the startups we build.
