- **Barefoot Ventures is forging** a new era in finance, investing capital in profitable companies driving human and planetary regeneration for all stakeholders. By redefining financial systems, rethinking investment strategies and sharing our regenerative business framework, we are helping to shift the finance paradigm towards integral and sustainable value creation. We support purposeful companies to thrive, profit and create enduring impact.
- **Our ambition** is to profitably invest at least 1 billion Euros into regeneration over the next 10 years. We exist to create a thriving capital ecosystem that can regenerate itself, communities and the earth for generations to come.
- **Our story.** Barefoot Ventures was born out of a fundamental belief in our collective potential and a heightened awareness of our individual responsibility. We are inspired by the work of regenerative pioneers in agriculture and economy, as well as the multitude of companies using business as a force for good. 
  At Barefoot Ventures, we see business as an opportunity for human and planetary regeneration, where finance is the catalyst and stakeholder profitability becomes the natural and healthy outcome of net positive contributions.
  We blend decades of experience across finance and business sectors with groundbreaking strategies and frameworks for regenerative companies and investors. This is our commitment to leave things better than we find them.
