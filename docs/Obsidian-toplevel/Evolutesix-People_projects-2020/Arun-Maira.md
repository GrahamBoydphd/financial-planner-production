Thank you for remembering me. I had ordered your book immediately after we met at the RIS LiFE meeting in Delhi and have read it too. 

I am grooving along with you in the same direction. I hope you will like my book. 

  

I have been in several discussions with David Sloan Wilson and Dennis Snower, separately and together, over the last few years. There is congruence in our intentions to change the paradigms of economics and governance, and in our outlines of the new paradigm. My great interest now is on "HOW to change the paradigm?" What are the institutional forces that resist it, and what are the movements from outside established "institutions" that can change it. There are some movements within that want change, such as the "conscious capitalism" movements that you mention in your YouTube video (which is brilliant!). However, these movements are trapped within established legal structures as you explain. Therefore, the energy to overcome the resistance to changing the basic paradigm itself must come from outside it. 

  

Your stage/level wise description of the evolution of "corporate" structures is brilliant. The highest stage is the "natural" stage of social structures which are in harmony with their natural environment. Therefore, we must learn from communities and societies that have not yet climbed up the corporate evolution ladder. Rather than trying to teach them how to become what we now think they should be. Thus, we must give more space, more voice, and more power to those who are not yet as "scientifically" smart and "developed" as we are, to accelerate the overall change in the paradigm. 

  

In the LiFE economy being propagated by RIS, there is a great emphasis on the development of "social enterprises". Which is the right way to go I think. However, I have two cautions. 

1. Too much energy is being expended in the creation of a "social stock exchange" to connect the new form enterprises with financial investors. They operate on different principles: "Property" rights on one side, and "Human" rights on the other. What are your views? (Already I am seeing the dilution of the human principle in favour of financial interests in the design of the structures of the emerging social stock exchange in India.)

2. The "conferences" in which LiFE  is being discussed are manifestations of the present power structures. (Including the LiFE conference in Delhi).They will not bring about the desired/required change fast enough. COP28 is another recent example.Therefore, I been urging all who care, including Dennis Snower, that the process of evolving the new ideas we need, and putting them into practice, must itself be reformed to accelerate the change.
