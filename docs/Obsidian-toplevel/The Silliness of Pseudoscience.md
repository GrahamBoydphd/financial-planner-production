The author of this concept, [David R Hawkins](https://email.notification.circle.so/c/eJwUzEuOtCAQAODTwE4DRSH0gsWf_DGznguYgvLVrdLx0cTbT_oCX8rrem3zeXczBwONV5JDg0k7ln3QrvHKGgQvp6CjbR5EpB0r0skiJ-sHxx6iMc6jnEPzaMBSxKSi4k6DMeiAvXMI3j0EKu7fS77XfjurSOk17vnauOJ-oGs5KxsRhzhEtJV9lifUK82LXMJ0nu9DmH8CWgFtKaUec-a9Jz7qlFcBLV3nlHcB7THlIqDV2nms_9Nn5u63-6HymrdD7mHcaaI15pu1QDV-_a8gPwH-AgAA__-Mq1Mp), exploited powerful meditation techniques that can genuinely help humans towards self-discovery, to build a profitable business out of conning unfulfilled people out of their money and their connection to reality. Hawkins chose to define ‘enlightenment’ in a manner that was completely disconnected from the real world.

I met a qualified practitioner of the Hawkins methods early on in my journey of realisation. I had deduced that humanity is trapped in an ecocidal direction with growth economics and pronatalism. I was looking for explanations as to why it was so difficult for most people to accept the scientific insights from I=PAT and data from the Global Footprint Network. He was the one of the most self-satisfied individuals that I have encountered on my journey. He was aware of the existential threats, and his answer was to teach the Hawkins way of dumbing down anxieties so they can have no effect on our behaviour or our life-choices. In the Hawkins worldview, once you are ‘enlightened’, you no longer feel worry or anger.

## **Abusing our Emotional Toolkit**

Hawkins’ work is not the only example of how we are taught to abuse our emotional toolkit. Hawkins was a product of our society. He was overlaying common socio-economic themes onto the deep traditions of meditation that underpin many oriental spiritual traditions. He came to conclusions about how we should use our emotional toolkit that simply reflected the society in which he grew up. He never questioned the wisdom of the socio-economic structure, he never recognised that our socio-economic model was the main cause of our emotional woes.

Hawkins probably genuinely believed that he was helping people. However, his ideas served to dig his audience deeper into an immature use of our emotional toolkit. He was greatly admired, and made a good deal of money selling his ideas, this all served to increase the illusion that he was speaking truthfully. That same illusion still applies to all the billionaires and oligarchs today.

## **Interplay between Emotions and Outcomes**

Let us analyse Hawkins’ summary of the interplay between emotions and outcomes:

When I look at the emotions that drive my own work, they are all at the lower end of this table from Hawkins. They are extremely powerful emotions without which I would not write anything at all. Hawkins was very unwise to dismiss these powerful emotions and to discourage his audience from using them to good creative effect.

- Shame — about my own responsibility for upholding the wealth hierarchy, and being part of a global ecocidal society
    
- Guilt — for not realising and acting sooner
    
- Hopelessness — this comes in waves, apathy never comes nowadays
    
- Grief , Regret — at the damage we are doing to Earth’s ecosystems
    
- Fear, Anxiety — at the lack of awareness in those around me
    
- Desire, Craving — for profound social change, I am enslaved to my work
    
- Anger, Hate — this comes less and less, as my work gets recognised
    
- Pride, Scorn — these come into play at little bit, I am proud when my work gets recognised, it does encourage me to continue. I get the impression, from listening to one of his lectures, that Hawkins was a very arrogant man, which is possibly why these emotions feature quite high on his list.
    

Right now, we are in man-made climate breakdown and ecosystems collapse. All the entries in the top half of Hawkins analysis are only likely to emerge as the consequence of a collective effort to overturn our toxic society that relies on ecocidal exponential growth and pronatalism.

I see the top half as a list of desirable outcomes, and the bottom half as the drivers. However, I would delete the top row entirely, as it is not a helpful aspiration. Achieving ‘completeness’ (I prefer ‘fulfilment’), joy and serenity, should be plenty for any one of us.

## **Spiritual Strength — Zen Buddhism**

My journey as a writer and an activist has required enormous emotional resilience, especially in the early years.

I found some insights from Zen Buddhism that helped me to keep my anxieties calm whilst I worked on raising awareness in others. Nowadays, before I invite sleep, I revisit six thoughts which were inspired by [Zen Buddhism](https://email.notification.circle.so/c/eJwUzMFurCAUgOGngZ0TPBwEFyxubjNN-xKTA0fUqcjE4hjfvnH7J_8XS877OtfzMbPX0Dkl2XcYW8ty8K3tnDIawcnJhxg0JELd95ySRSJ0yTnjeiAXbJSz7_oODAWMKih-tKA1WmBnLYKzvUDFw2spZx7W2gSKP-NW9pUbHhLtS21MQEwhBTSNeR5PuGWaF7n4qdbXr9D_BNwF3I_juJ1lr3sYbrHkq1CNk9D3t9AfbTz4e_yk7f-X3Py40UQ5lJNbgWq8vOuRbw9_AQAA___Z0E33). I have adapted these thoughts to reflect my personal ambitions for humanity:

- Today is over, I must let go of today’s events
    
- I forgive myself and others for the man-made ecological destruction that continued throughout today
    
- I am grateful for everything that I learned or achieved today
    
- Today I continued to play my part in the push for radical change
    
- Tomorrow is a new day, we shall be further along in the mindset shift
    
- I wish everyone acute awareness of the escalating man-made existential threats. I wish everyone the moral courage to accept that reality and to find their own purpose within that challenging context.
