### Dana summary of ergodic investing
In the current investment world, it is assumed the world is mechanical, predictable, and even if things change, they will even out over time (=ergodic state of the world). 
However, in a VUCA (or even nowadays BANI) world this approach is not only outdated but outright dangerous as it is high risk, short-sighted, blind to social & environmental risks and concerns and leads to misallocation of capital and fragile economies. 
The world is non-ergodic (means alive and moving all the time at high speed and unpredictable), it hence needs an entirely new approach to investing which not only reduces risks but also builds resilient economies. This approach embraces complex systems thinking, is already proven mathematically and is the formula to use to safeguard your portfolios and for a regenerative future.
