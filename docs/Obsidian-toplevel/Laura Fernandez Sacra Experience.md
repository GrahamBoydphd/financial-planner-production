
A client is doing a superb job of that most challenging startup task. She's been doing lots of small prototypes to get to product market fit before burning up lots of resources going global. Exactly the right approach! 

She's had chances to scale with some of the products she's developed. But she's resisted the pressure from other startup advisors because she's clear that she's not alive merely make lots of money selling cute products. She's on a mission to break dysfunctional patterns.  Especially the dysfunctional patterns that still lead, in so many parts of the world, to some groups of people being invisible. Having their agency reduced or eliminated. 

Breaking these dysfunctional patterns requires strong company foundations. The foundations themselves must walk the talk of agency, beyond what cooperatives, public benefit corporations, employee or steward owned etc. can deliver. 

 So today we've started work on the crux stone of our agentic incorporation: the external context and need. Because the purpose follows that. To get there we've talked about her life story, going back to her great grandmother, to understand where she is now. Her deepest level why. Why is solving this problem so important to her. 

 Now we're about to tell the story of her possible futures, those of the company she's founded, and how they're related. 

 From that we'll have the essence of the company. And the essence, not the purpose, is what really matters in building strong foundations for disruptive, regenerative, ecosystem ready companies.
